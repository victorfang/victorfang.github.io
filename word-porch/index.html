A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Make yourself
at home.

Do I need to be good at word games?

Just bring a little curiosity. Make words from the letters you’re given, one letter tile at a time. Free clues can help you along, and there’s no timer.

What can I play for free?

Ten destinations are included free. The game also has a daily puzzle and free practice. Normal word submissions use points; undo, editing, and clues are free. Optional points and extra destination collections appear in the app when store products are available.

Does the game have advertisements?

The free version includes ads. Owning a destination pack removes the banner and automatic ads between puzzles. Optional rewarded videos can provide extra points when available.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. The installed app bundles its puzzles and destination artwork, so you can play offline. Downloading the app, making or restoring purchases, and loading optional ads need an internet connection.

Can I make the words easier to read?

Yes. Open the Word Porch menu in the game and turn on Large text. There’s also a Quiet motion setting. On this page, use the Larger text button at the top, or your browser’s zoom controls.

Will the game remember where I stopped?

Your puzzle progress and postcard collection are saved on your device. You can return to an unfinished puzzle later. Automatic syncing between devices isn’t included; the game offers progress backup and import.
