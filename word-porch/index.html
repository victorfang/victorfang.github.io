GOOD TO KNOW

Make yourself
at home.

Is it easy to start?

Yes. Tap letters to make words. Clues are free, and there’s no timer.

What’s free?

Ten destinations, a daily puzzle, and practice. Normal word submissions use points; editing and clues are free. Extra points and destination packs are optional.

Are there ads?

Free play includes ads. A destination pack removes banners and automatic ads. Optional videos can earn points.

Can I play offline?

Yes, once installed. Downloads, purchases, restores, and ads need internet.

Can I use larger text?

Yes. Choose Large text or Quiet motion in the game menu. On this page, use Larger text at the top.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Backup and import are available; automatic syncing between devices isn’t included.
