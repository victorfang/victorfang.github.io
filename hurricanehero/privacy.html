PRIVACY · FANG VENTURES

Your adventure stays on your device.

Updated October 19, 2026

Hurricane Hero is published by Fang Ventures. This notice describes our browser game and iPhone app, local progress, optional sharing, Apple purchases and Google advertising.

For support or privacy questions, contact Fang Ventures at fangventures.hq@gmail.com.

Game progress and preferences

The game stores your mission progress, stars, best scores by difficulty, local leaderboard entries, city discoveries and difficulty stamps, optional hero name, selected cartoon avatar, and settings such as audio preferences on your device. The browser uses local storage; the iPhone app uses its local web content storage. The game has no account, cloud save service, or online leaderboard.

These records are not sent to a game-operated server. You can remove browser data using your browser’s website-data controls. For the iPhone app, use the device’s app-data or app-removal controls. Device backup and restore settings may affect copies retained by your operating system.

Audio and permissions

Game audio uses bundled music and sound files plus locally generated effects. The landing screen requests music playback, falls back to a tap where required, and remembers your mute and volume choices. It does not use your microphone, record speech, or upload audio. The game does not use your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone. Saving a shared card may use add-only Photos access; the game does not read your photo library. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or access to the advertising identifier.

Advertising on iPhone

The website shows a labeled house-ad preview and does not load AdMob. The iPhone app integrates Google AdMob banner ads below the game and full-screen ads at completed-stage breaks. An active Apple-verified Ad-Free subscription removes both formats. Debug builds use Google test ads; production builds use the publisher’s AdMob ad units.

Before requesting ads, the app checks Google’s User Messaging Platform consent status and presents required privacy messages. When available, you can reopen Privacy options in the Ad-Free settings. Ad availability depends on consent status, network access and inventory; a missing ad does not block gameplay.

Google’s advertising SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics and performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Other advertising data can still be processed without access to the advertising identifier. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s SDK data disclosures.

Apple purchases and subscription status

The browser cannot accept payments. On iPhone, Apple StoreKit handles purchases and supplies verified transaction and subscription status to the app. The app uses verified Apple transaction information to decide whether Ad-Free is active; a website setting cannot grant a paid entitlement.

The game does not receive your payment card details. Local StoreKit and Apple sandbox transactions are test transactions. Where a production subscription is available, Apple’s purchase sheet will show its price and terms before confirmation. Apple’s handling of purchase and account data is described in Apple’s privacy information.

Shield inventory

The browser saves your starter-shield balance in local storage. The iPhone app saves shield balances, processed Apple transaction identifiers and pending activations in the device Keychain to prevent duplicate delivery and recover interrupted activations. This wallet is local to the device, is not synced to iCloud or a game server, and may remain in the Keychain after app removal. Test and production wallets are separate.

Apple handles consumable shield-pack payments. The app credits shields only from verified Apple transactions and does not receive your card details.

Sharing is your choice

An arcade friend challenge contains the starting region, layout seed, difficulty and score to beat. Tutorial challenges contain the mission and score. Hero cards include game artwork and run statistics; difficulty badges and city discovery cards can also be shared. The game creates the card locally. It only opens a share sheet, copies a link, or saves a card when you request that action. Your optional hero name is not added to challenge links.

If you send a link or card through another app, that service receives the information you choose to share and handles it under its own privacy practices. There are no automatic social posts or contact uploads.

Website delivery and external links

The public web host receives ordinary request information, such as an IP address and browser headers, to deliver the website. The website has no advertising pixels, game analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The native advertising SDK processes its own advertising and diagnostic information as described above. Core game art, code and audio do not require an external asset service.

Following a source, privacy or support link opens an external website. That website may process data according to its own policies. The iPhone app opens external web links in the system browser rather than loading them into its trusted game view.
