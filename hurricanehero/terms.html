Playing the game

Hurricane Hero is a casual simulation. The campaign’s weather, routes, points and challenges are simplified and fictional. The history archive identifies recorded hurricane tracks separately. The game is for entertainment and learning; it is not a forecast or an emergency-planning tool.

The current campaign, history archive, local rankings and friend challenges remain available without an Ad-Free subscription. Scores and friend challenges use an honor system and have no cash value or prizes.

Hurricane Hero Ad-Free

Ad-Free is an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription that removes banner and full-screen ads while the Apple-verified subscription entitlement is active. It does not unlock extra missions, increase scores, or guarantee a leaderboard position.

Where Ad-Free is available, the app and Apple purchase sheet show the current localized monthly price before you confirm. Prices may vary by storefront and currency.

The website cannot take a payment or activate a subscription. The iPhone test build uses local StoreKit or Apple sandbox transactions when configured for testing. Test transactions do not create a paid web subscription. No subscription begins merely by opening the paywall or pressing a disabled web button.

Storm shields

Each new device wallet starts with 10 free shields. Activating one consumes it and reduces direct and blast missile damage by 50% for 15 seconds of active arcade gameplay. Pausing pauses the timer. Shields do not stack; an activated shield ends when its timer or the round ends. Shields do not reduce weather damage.

The iOS consumable packs contain 10, 100 or 1,000 shields. Available products show Apple’s localized price before checkout. Packs are consumable purchases, not subscriptions, and do not remove ads. The website cannot accept shield payments.

Unused shields do not expire. Inventory is stored on this device, without account or cross-device synchronization. Used shields cannot be restored. Subscription restoration does not refill consumables; Check purchases & balance can recover an unfinished purchase on the same device. If Apple revokes a delivered purchase, the app deducts the refunded pack from the remaining balance, without making it negative.

Renewal, cancellation and restoration

For an Apple subscription, payment is charged to your Apple Account after you confirm. The subscription renews monthly at the price shown by Apple unless you cancel before renewal. Manage or cancel it through Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation normally stops a future renewal; entitlement remains governed by Apple’s current subscription status.

The app provides Restore purchases and Manage subscription actions in the iPhone build. Restoration requires an eligible active entitlement for your Apple Account. Ad-Free status can change when a subscription expires, is refunded, or is revoked; the app refreshes verified status accordingly.

Apple handles billing and refund requests. See Apple’s cancellation instructions and Apple’s refund information. App Store distribution is also subject to the applicable Apple license terms, including Apple’s standard licensed application agreement where it applies.

Local progress and sharing

Progress, difficulty records, city discoveries and passport stamps are stored on your device. There is no account or cloud-save guarantee. Clearing website data, removing the app, changing devices, or testing a new build may affect saved progress.

You control whether to share a challenge link or hero card. The game does not automatically message friends or publish results. Shared scores are not verified global rankings.
