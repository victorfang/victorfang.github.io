Game progress stays on your device.

Teslaro 3D stores selected cars, cities and ramps, best stars, introduction progress, and preferences such as sound and reduced motion locally. The browser uses website storage; the iPhone and iPad app uses local web-content storage. App progress is separate from browser progress.

There is no Teslaro game account, publisher-operated cloud save or online leaderboard. These local gameplay records are not uploaded to a game-operated server. Advertising, purchases, website delivery and support communications involve other data as described below.

Game audio uses bundled music, sound files and locally generated effects. Core gameplay does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

Advertising and privacy choices

The iPhone and iPad app integrates Google AdMob banner ads and paced full-screen ads between completed flights. The browser game does not load native AdMob. Ads require a network connection and availability; unavailable ads do not prevent core gameplay.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent information and present required privacy messages before requesting eligible ads. When Google makes privacy options available, open the Teslaro menu and choose Ad privacy to reopen those choices.

Google’s advertising and consent SDKs may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, product interaction information, crash information, performance information and other diagnostics. These may be used for advertising, measurement, consent handling and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the SDK, your region, available consent choices and applicable provider settings.

For Google’s handling of this information and available controls, see Google’s privacy policy, Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures and Google My Ad Center. Consent choices do not remove all advertising or diagnostic data processing.

Apple purchases and ownership

Midnight Gold is an optional one-time, non-consumable cosmetic car unlock. Apple StoreKit handles checkout and provides verified transaction and entitlement information so the app can determine ownership, restore purchases and respond to refunds or revocations. Midnight Gold has the same flight handling as Ice Blue and does not remove ads.

The game does not receive your payment-card details. Apple shows the current localized price before you confirm a purchase. The browser cannot complete this purchase. TestFlight and sandbox transactions are handled under Apple’s testing rules.

Apple processes purchase and account information under Apple’s privacy policy. For purchase help, see our purchase and restoration guide.

Sharing is your choice.

The game creates flight-report pictures on your device using game artwork, snapshots from your actual flight, the selected city and course, and flight statistics. Choosing Share, Save PNG, or a social option opens the available system share sheet, download action or external composer.

The game does not automatically publish posts or upload contacts. If you choose to send a report, picture or link through another service, that service receives what you send and handles it under its own privacy practices. Available sharing destinations and save options depend on your device and installed apps.

Website delivery and external links

This public information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and its delivery infrastructure receive ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser headers, to deliver pages and operate the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

This information website has no advertising SDK, analytics scripts, contact form, remote font service or embedded social tracker. It uses local images and styles. The native game’s advertising SDK has its own processing as described above.

Links to Apple, Google, GitHub or another service open that provider’s website. Its own policies apply to information it receives.

Support and privacy messages

If you email or call Fang Ventures, we receive the contact information and message details you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, device information or screenshots. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Email and telephone providers also handle communications under their own policies.

Please send only information needed for your request. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information. Contact us if you have questions about information you previously sent or want to request its deletion, subject to any applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Deleting local data and device backups

Browser website-data controls can remove browser progress and preferences. Deleting the native app can remove its local game data; offloading an app may retain data. Device and cloud backup settings may retain copies or restore data later. Clearing local data can erase your progress, and we do not operate a game account database from which to recover it.

Deleting local game data does not delete records held independently by Apple, Google, GitHub or your communications provider. Use the relevant provider’s privacy controls for those records. A verified Midnight Gold purchase may be restored through Apple using the same Apple Account; restoration does not restore local stars or other game progress.
