Game progress stays on your device.

Teslaro 3D stores selected cars, cities and ramps, best stars, introduction progress, the game-points total, one banked next-flight fuel bonus, and preferences such as sound, night mode and reduced motion locally. The browser uses website storage; the iPhone and iPad app uses local web-content storage. App progress is separate from browser progress.

The new U-turn courses use the same local course/star records; existing course results remain on the device. There is no Teslaro game account, publisher-operated cloud save or online leaderboard. These local gameplay records are not uploaded to a game-operated server. Advertising, purchases, website delivery and support communications involve other data as described below.

Game audio uses bundled music, sound files and locally generated effects. Core gameplay does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

Advertising and privacy choices

The iPhone and iPad app integrates Google AdMob for paced interstitials after successfully completed ramps and optional rewarded videos. There are no banner ads. Interstitials may appear only when continuing from a successful flight report, subject to a three-completion and 120-second pacing gate; failed flights do not create an interstitial opportunity. The browser game does not load native AdMob. Ads require a network connection and availability; unavailable ads do not prevent core gameplay.

The app waits for verified native Apple purchase-entitlement information before requesting ads or advertising consent. Ad-supported players use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent information and present required privacy messages before eligible ads. When Google makes privacy options available, open Teslaro 3D Menu → Settings → Ad privacy to reopen those choices.

An optional completed video can award 100 noncash game points and one 25% next-flight fuel bonus only after the native advertising SDK confirms the earned result. The points total and unused fuel bonus are stored locally. These rewards do not unlock paid cars or establish purchase ownership. A skipped or unavailable video does not grant a reward.

Google’s advertising and consent SDKs may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, product interaction information, crash information, performance information and other diagnostics. These may be used for advertising, measurement, consent handling and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the SDK, your region, available consent choices and applicable provider settings.

For Google’s handling of this information and available controls, see Google’s privacy policy, Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures and Google My Ad Center. Consent choices do not remove all advertising or diagnostic data processing.

Apple purchases and ownership

The garage has five free 1× cars and seven separate optional one-time, non-consumable car upgrades. Relative to the free baseline, Ice Blue provides 2× rocket boost, fuel capacity, steering power and stabilization assistance. Midnight Gold, Tokyo Neon, Singapore Ion and Austin Longhorn each provide 3× all four; Dubai Gold and Platinum X each provide 5× all four. Apple StoreKit handles checkout and provides verified transaction and entitlement information so the app can determine ownership, restore purchases and respond to refunds or revocations. Each purchase unlocks only the matching car and does not remove ads. Existing verified Midnight Gold and Dubai Gold purchases continue to grant access to those cars.

Remove all ads is an eighth, separate optional one-time non-consumable Apple product with a $10 USD base price; Apple supplies the current localized checkout price. After verified current ownership, the app suppresses new ad requests, interstitials and optional rewarded-video offers. An already presented SDK sheet completes its normal dismissal flow. Previously earned points and banked fuel remain. Removing ads grants no cars, and the seven car products grant no ad-removal entitlement.

Use Restore purchases in Settings or Cars with the same Apple Account to restore eligible products. Native StoreKit entitlement information controls ownership; a local preference or a purchase-result message alone does not. Verified refunds or revocations remove the corresponding product access. Refunding Remove all ads re-enables the normal ad-supported rules after verification. Restoration does not recover local stars, points or unused fuel bonuses.

The game does not receive your payment-card details. Apple shows the current localized price before you confirm a purchase. The browser cannot complete this purchase. TestFlight and sandbox transactions are handled under Apple’s testing rules.

Apple processes purchase and account information under Apple’s privacy policy. For purchase help, see our purchase and restoration guide.

Sharing is your choice.

The game creates flight-report pictures on your device using game artwork, snapshots from your actual flight, the selected city and course, and flight statistics. Choosing Share, Save PNG, or a social option opens the available system share sheet, download action or external composer.

The game does not automatically publish posts or upload contacts. If you choose to send a report, picture or link through another service, that service receives what you send and handles it under its own privacy practices. Available sharing destinations and save options depend on your device and installed apps.

Website delivery and external links

This public information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and its delivery infrastructure receive ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser headers, to deliver pages and operate the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

This information website has no advertising SDK, analytics scripts, contact form, remote font service or embedded social tracker. It uses local images and styles. The native game’s advertising SDK has its own processing as described above.

Links to Apple, Google, GitHub or another service open that provider’s website. Its own policies apply to information it receives.

Support and privacy messages

If you email or call Fang Ventures, we receive the contact information and message details you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, device information or screenshots. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Email and telephone providers also handle communications under their own policies.

Please send only information needed for your request. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information. Contact us if you have questions about information you previously sent or want to request its deletion, subject to any applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Deleting local data and device backups

Browser website-data controls can remove browser progress and preferences. Deleting the native app can remove its local game data; offloading an app may retain data. Device and cloud backup settings may retain copies or restore data later. Clearing local data can erase your progress, and we do not operate a game account database from which to recover it.

Deleting local game data does not delete records held independently by Apple, Google, GitHub or your communications provider. Use the relevant provider’s privacy controls for those records. Verified premium-car and Remove all ads purchases may be restored through Apple using the same Apple Account; restoration does not restore local stars, points, unused fuel bonuses or other game progress.
