How do the rocket controls work?

Play in landscape orientation. Choose a city and ramp, then tap Fly. The countdown shows 3, 2, 1, then Go!: two seconds of course overview and one second approaching the car. After takeoff, use the two controls to balance your car in the air.

Left · Nose ↑

Fire the rear rockets to lift the nose.

Right · Nose ↓

Fire the front rockets to lower the nose.

Use short taps to save fuel. Both controls provide lift while helping you keep a steady angle. Aim to land wheels-down on the target pad. Open Rocket Car Landing Menu → Tips to revisit the instructions. The menu also has Cars, Cities, Ramps and Settings.

First two ramps: Beginner landing assist is on by default. Hold both controls to level the car and manage landing thrust. It helps every car, including the stronger paid models, without changing their maximum performance. A gentle rear-wheel touch while rolling off the crest no longer ends the flight; body and hard impacts still count as crashes.

On a keyboard: Space launches, A or ← fires the rear rockets, D or → fires the front rockets, and R retries.

Which ramp should I try next?

There are 88 courses across 11 cities, with eight courses per city drawn from 22 original ramp designs. Each city keeps its six established courses, beginning with two short lessons: Mellow has a 12 m ramp and Crescent has a 16 m ramp. Four longer 32–82 m forward courses follow, then the two new left-landing U-turn challenges.

The ramp picker shows the driving length, horizontal gap from takeoff to the near pad edge, difficulty and an advisory 1×, 2× or 3× rocket recommendation. U-ramp length measures the curved driving surface. Every car may attempt every course; a recommendation does not lock a ramp, grant a paid car or start a purchase. Aero and the other free cars provide 1× power; Ice Blue provides 2×, Midnight Gold 3×, and Dubai Gold/Platinum X 5×. Higher-power cars require shorter, carefully timed taps. Advanced and Expert courses stay manual.

Guided ramp driving is about twice as fast as before. Entry, mid and premium cars travel the ramp 10%, 20% and 30% faster than free cars; these approach bonuses are separate from their existing 2×, 3× and 5× rocket/fuel performance and unchanged prices. The car settles to the same takeoff state, so airborne control and landing rules remain the same. A glowing rear booster during the ramp drive is cosmetic: it uses no fuel and grants no extra airborne thrust.

Look at the target before launching. The forward Golden Crown keeps its 82 m ramp and pad beginning 76 m beyond the crest. The U courses return to a separate pad on the LEFT, outside the ramp and starting runway. Course names, previews and the challenge label show the difference. Existing course stars and selections remain on your device.

How do U-turn backflips work?

Open Rocket Car Landing Menu → Ramps, then tap ↶ U ramps to find the new cards at positions 7 and 8. The shortcut selects a preview and scrolls it into view; Fly still starts the countdown.

  • U Boomerang: a 16 m-radius curve turning through 140°, about 39 m of curved driving surface. Free 1× Aero is the recommendation.
  • U Switchback: a 22 m-radius curve turning through 150°, about 58 m of curved driving surface. 2× Ice Blue is the recommendation. Every car may still try.

Climb the U, let its lip launch you back toward the LEFT, finish the backward rotation and touch down wheels-first on the bright left pad. The curve carries its turning motion into flight. Save fuel for leveling and a softer touchdown. Use short rear/front rocket taps to manage pitch and both controls for lift and leveling; stronger cars need gentler taps. The two U courses are manual, even when Beginner landing assist is on.

The camera follows the turning ramp, and a high-contrast LANDING arrow with a metre distance points toward the pad when it is offscreen. It disappears when the pad is in view or gameplay is paused. Timing varies with city wind, wet grip and car power: adjust your coast and bursts rather than expecting one identical sequence everywhere. Night mode keeps the car, ramp and landing cues bright.

Car tiers, purchases and restoration

The garage has 15 original cars, arranged from free coupes to premium rocket racers. All performance comparisons use the free 1× baseline.

  • Free · 1×: Aero, Comet, Orbit, Miami Vice and Paris Éclair. Aero is the starting car. All five have identical rocket boost, fuel capacity, steering power and stabilization assistance, with different body, roof, paint and trim features.
  • Entry · $1 USD · 2×: Ice Blue, the cyan-accented roadster; red Fang S, an original fastback; and gray Fang Y, an original crossover. All four performance values are twice the free baseline.
  • Mid · $5 USD each · 3×: Midnight Gold, navy Fang Punk, Tokyo Neon, Singapore Ion and Austin Longhorn. Each has three times the free baseline for all four values.
  • Premium · $9 USD each · 5×: Dubai Gold and Platinum X. Both have five times the free baseline for all four values. Platinum X adds a metallic platinum finish and violet aero blades.

Stronger rockets accelerate the car harder during a burn, and larger tanks allow longer burns at the same input. The listed multipliers describe boost, fuel, steering and assist; they do not promise the same multiple of speed or flight time. Course geometry and landing rules are shared. On manual Advanced and Expert courses, higher-power cars, especially the 5× models, reward short taps and careful timing.

Each paid car is a separate optional one-time, non-consumable upgrade. The prices above are USD base prices; Apple shows your current localized price before you confirm when the product is available. In the native iPhone or iPad app, open Cars, select a car and use its purchase button. Opening the garage or previewing a car does not buy it. A purchase unlocks only that car and does not remove ads.

Already own Midnight Gold or Dubai Gold? Your existing verified purchase keeps access to that same car under the updated lineup. You do not need to buy it again. Use Restore purchases in the garage with the same Apple Account if it appears locked, including after reinstalling or on another supported device. Apple must verify ownership. Restoration does not recover local stars, game points or banked fuel, and a refunded or revoked purchase loses access.

Apple handles billing and refund requests. There is no recurring subscription. Purchases in TestFlight follow Apple’s test-purchase rules and are separate from production ownership.

Why is a car locked or checkout unavailable?

The browser supports locked premium-car previews and cannot complete Apple purchases. On iPhone or iPad, a purchase can be unavailable if the product is not offered yet, Apple cannot load it, connectivity is unavailable, or purchases are restricted on the device. Check your connection, reopen the app and try again. Do not buy repeatedly to resolve a delivery problem; try Restore purchases first if you already own that car.

All ten paid cars have separate Apple products. A lock means the selected car has no verified current entitlement. Buying one car does not unlock any other car. If you already own the selected car, restore with the same Apple Account and wait for Apple to verify it. Product availability depends on the App Store configuration and review status; a displayed price alone does not establish ownership. If a saved paid-car choice is unavailable, the game uses Aero while your verified purchases are restored.

Can I play offline? Where is my progress?

The installed iPhone and iPad game bundles its gameplay resources and works offline. Ads, App Store transactions and external links need an internet connection. No game account is required.

Selected cars, cities, ramps, stars, your game-points total, a banked next-flight fuel bonus and settings stay on your device. Browser progress is separate from native app progress. There is no cloud-save or cross-device progress service. Redesigned courses earn a fresh set of stars; previous course records remain stored on your device. Clearing website data or deleting the app can erase local progress; device backups may restore data according to your operating-system settings. Keep this in mind before reinstalling to troubleshoot.

Ads and privacy choices

The native iPhone and iPad app has no banner ads. Paced full-screen interstitials may appear only after a successfully completed ramp, when you tap Retry or Next to continue. Failed flights do not create an interstitial opportunity. Interstitial pacing requires at least three successful ramp completions since the last full-screen ad and a 120-second interval from session start or the previous full-screen ad. Watching an optional rewarded video also restarts that interval and completion count.

Ads depend on connectivity, consent status and inventory. An unavailable ad does not delay the next flight or block core gameplay. The browser game does not request native AdMob ads.

Required advertising privacy messages are handled through Google UMP. When available for an ad-supported player, open Rocket Car Landing Menu → Settings → Ad privacy to revisit privacy choices. Read our privacy policy for information about advertising providers, purchases, local game data and support messages.

Optional videos, game points and bonus fuel

When a video is available, you may choose Watch video · +100 points +25% fuel from the menu or Settings → Ads & points, or the compact 🎬 +100 · +25% ⛽ offer in the flight-report header. A matching native SDK-earned result awards 100 game points and banks 25% extra fuel for your next flight. Skipping, dismissing before earning or failing to load a video does not grant the reward. Normal Play and Retry remain available without watching.

Only one fuel bonus can be banked at a time; it cannot stack. It survives menu, city and car changes, then is spent when the next flight actually begins after the countdown. The bonus extends available fuel without changing your selected car or rocket power.

Points and the banked fuel bonus are local, noncash game progress. Points cannot be exchanged for money, paid cars or Remove all ads. A video never grants StoreKit ownership. You can see your points in Settings; deleting local game data can erase points and unused fuel bonuses.

Remove all ads — optional one-time purchase

Open Rocket Car Landing Menu → Settings → Ads & points to find Remove all ads. It is a separate optional, non-consumable Apple purchase with a $10 USD base price. Apple shows the current localized price before confirmation when the product is available. There is no recurring subscription.

After Apple verifies current ownership, the app stops new ad requests, suppresses interstitials and removes optional rewarded-video offers as well. A full-screen ad already presented finishes its normal dismissal flow. Previously earned game points and banked fuel remain available. Removing ads does not unlock any car or grant new video rewards.

Use Restore purchases in Settings or Cars with the same Apple Account after reinstalling or on another supported device. The app waits for verified entitlement information before requesting ads. A refunded or revoked Remove all ads purchase returns the app to the normal ad-supported rules once Apple verifies that change. The ten paid-car products remain separate: buying a car does not remove ads, and removing ads does not unlock a car.

If the price or buy button is unavailable, check your connection and device purchase restrictions and try again later. Do not buy again to fix a missing entitlement; restore first. Apple handles billing and refund requests. Restore purchases restores verified products, not local stars, points or unused fuel bonuses.

Settings, audio and display

Open Rocket Car Landing Menu → Settings for audio, graphics, vibration and credits. Use the separate Music and Effects switches; both choices are remembered. The speaker button controls sound effects. Check your device’s media volume and tap once to allow audio playback. Music loops across menus, ramps and results: the default Hurricane Hero groove changes to the Middle East track in Dubai and the Asia track in Tokyo or Singapore, then returns to the default elsewhere. Completing a ramp plays the winning sound when effects are on.

Backgrounding, interruptions, full-screen ads and consent forms suspend audio; returning respects your settings.

Under Flight, Beginner landing assist is on by default for Mellow and Crescent, the first two ramps in each city. Hold both buttons for assisted landing thrust. Turn it off for manual flight. Single-button taps and all Advanced and Expert ramps use manual power regardless of this switch. Your choice is remembered.

Under Graphics, choose High for richer lighting or Balanced for a lighter graphics mode. Turn on Reduce motion to reduce motion and animated effects. The Vibration switch controls feedback on supported iPhones. These preferences stay on your device.

Turn your iPhone or iPad sideways to play. The portrait screen pauses the game and asks you to rotate. Use Back to menu in Settings when you are finished, then Play to return to the game.

How do I use or share a flight report?

The landscape report keeps your character artwork, a short reaction, fuel used, landing tilt and shock, and three moments from your actual flight. Missing captures are shown honestly rather than replaced. Its rating out of five stars reflects touchdown tilt and vertical impact; a crash or missing touchdown shows zero. This display rating is separate from your saved three-star course progress, which is unchanged.

Six controls appear on the right: Retry, Next, Share, City, Ramp and Cars. Retry repeats this ramp. Next advances to the next local course even after a crash; on the last course it opens the ramp picker. City, Ramp and Cars open their pickers directly without requesting an ad. Closing an unchanged City picker returns to the same report. Use X to close the report.

Choose Share to open the system share option or Save PNG. The picture includes the same five-star rating and actual snapshots. Available destinations depend on your device, browser and installed apps. X and Facebook open a link composer; they do not automatically attach the report image. To share the image manually, save it first and attach it in your chosen app. The game never publishes a post without your action.

If something still goes wrong, email Fang Ventures with your device, app version and the steps that led to the problem.
