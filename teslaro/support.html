How do the rocket controls work?

Play in landscape orientation. Choose a city and ramp, then tap Fly. After the countdown, use the two controls to balance your car in the air.

Left · Nose ↑

Fire the rear rockets to lift the nose.

Right · Nose ↓

Fire the front rockets to lower the nose.

Use short taps to save fuel. Both controls provide lift while helping you keep a steady angle. Aim to land wheels-down on the target pad. Open How to in the Teslaro menu to revisit the instructions.

On a keyboard: Space launches, A or ← fires the rear rockets, D or → fires the front rockets, and R retries.

Premium car purchases and restoration

Ice Blue is free. Midnight Gold has 2× Ice Blue's rocket boost, fuel capacity, steering power and stabilization assistance; Dubai Gold has 3× all four. Each is a separate optional one-time, non-consumable upgrade with a USD 10.00 base price. Neither purchase removes ads or unlocks the other car. In the native iPhone or iPad app, open Cars, select Midnight Gold or Dubai Gold, and use that car's purchase button when available. Apple shows your current localized price before you confirm. Opening the garage does not make a purchase.

To recover an existing purchase after reinstalling or on another supported device, use Restore purchases in the garage with the same Apple Account used to buy it. Ownership must be verified by Apple. Restoration does not recover local game progress. A refunded or revoked purchase loses access.

Apple handles billing and refund requests. Both are one-time purchases, with no recurring subscription. Purchases in TestFlight follow Apple’s test-purchase rules and are separate from production ownership.

Why is a car locked or checkout unavailable?

The browser supports locked premium-car previews and cannot complete Apple purchases. On iPhone or iPad, a purchase can be unavailable if the product is not offered yet, Apple cannot load it, connectivity is unavailable, or purchases are restricted on the device. Check your connection, reopen the app and try again. Do not buy repeatedly to resolve a delivery problem; try Restore purchases first if you already own that car.

Midnight Gold and Dubai Gold have separate Apple products. A lock means the selected car has no verified current entitlement. Buying one car does not unlock the other. If you already own the selected car, restore with the same Apple Account and wait for Apple to verify it. Product availability depends on the App Store configuration and review status; a displayed price alone does not establish ownership.

Can I play offline? Where is my progress?

The installed iPhone and iPad game bundles its gameplay resources and works offline. Ads, App Store transactions and external links need an internet connection. No game account is required.

Selected cars, cities, ramps, stars and settings stay on your device. Browser progress is separate from native app progress. There is no cloud-save or cross-device progress service. Clearing website data or deleting the app can erase local progress; device backups may restore data according to your operating-system settings. Keep this in mind before reinstalling to troubleshoot.

Ads and privacy choices

The native app uses Google AdMob banners and paced full-screen ads between completed flights. Ad availability depends on connectivity, consent status and inventory. Missing ads do not block core gameplay.

Required privacy messages are handled through Google UMP. When available, open the Teslaro menu and choose Ad privacy to revisit privacy choices. Read our privacy policy for information about the game, advertising providers, purchases and support messages.

Sound, orientation and reduced motion

Use the sound control or the Teslaro menu’s Sound switch and check your device’s media volume. Browsers may need a tap before audio starts. The native game pauses when interrupted or while a full-screen ad or consent form is open.

Turn your iPhone or iPad sideways to play. The portrait screen pauses the game and asks you to rotate. Turn on Calm in the Teslaro menu to reduce motion and animated effects.

How do I save or share a flight report?

After a flight, open Share on the flight report. The report includes snapshots from your actual run. Choose the system share option or Save PNG. Available destinations depend on your device, browser and installed apps.

X and Facebook options open a link composer; they do not automatically attach the report image. To share the image manually, save it first and attach it in your chosen app. The game does not publish a post without your action.

If something still goes wrong, email Fang Ventures with your device, app version and the steps that led to the problem.
