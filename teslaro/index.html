01 / YOUR RIDE

Start with Aero.

Meet fictional inventor Dr. Fang and choose from 15 original cars. Five Entry coupes are free. Unlock Mid cars for 100 Gold, Advanced cars for 500 Gold or Premium Dubai Gold and Platinum X for 1,000 Gold. Start with 25 welcome Gold and earn more with first successful and perfect course landings. Optional Apple Gold packs are available in the iPhone and iPad app when configured. Compare the lineup and recovery limits.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Earn the landing.

Explore 88 courses across 11 cities, with eight courses per city and 22 original ramp styles. Start with two short lessons, then try longer jumps and the new U Boomerang and U Switchback: climb the curve, flip backward and land on the LEFT pad. Learn the U-turn challenge.

03 / YOUR FLIGHT

Share the proof.

Earn stars and improve your personal bests. Save or share a flight report with snapshots from your actual run. Sharing happens only when you choose it.
