A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Before you glow.

How can I play?

Play Arrow Neon in your browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. No download or account is required. An iPhone and iPad release is coming soon; the browser version is available now.

Is there a time limit?

There is no countdown. The clock records your active play time and personal best, but time never affects your stars or lives. Take a breath and find your next move.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Open the browser game online once and let it finish loading. Its core puzzles, bundled artwork, and sounds are saved for offline play. External links, ads, and Apple purchases require a connection.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

The browser version uses clearly labeled ad and purchase simulations with no real charges. The native app includes ads and optional life packs and Remove Ads purchases. Remove Ads hides banners and interstitials; rewarded videos remain optional. Main-game hints use points, while advanced-practice hints and retries are free.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are stored on your device. Profiles are optional. There is no account or cloud sync, and clearing browser or app data can remove your progress.

How do I get help?

Email fangventures.hq@gmail.com with “Arrow Neon” and your device or browser version. We'd love to help you find your way.
