A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Before you glow.

How can I play?

Arrow Neon is coming soon exclusively to the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad. Download the app once it is released to begin your journey. No account is required.

Is there a time limit?

There is no countdown. The clock records your active play time and personal best, but time never affects your stars or lives. Take a breath and find your next move.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Core puzzles, artwork, and sounds are bundled in the iPhone and iPad app and work offline after installation. External links, ads, and Apple purchases require a connection.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

The app includes ads and optional life packs and Remove Ads purchases through Apple. Remove Ads hides banners and interstitials; rewarded videos remain optional. Main-game hints use points, while advanced-practice hints and retries are free.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are stored on your device. Profiles are optional. There is no account or cloud sync, and deleting app data can remove your progress.

How do I get help?

Email fangventures.hq@gmail.com with “Arrow Neon” and your device and app version. We'd love to help you find your way.
