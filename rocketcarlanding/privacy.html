Game progress stays on your device.

Rocket Car Landing stores selected cars, cities and ramps, best stars, introduction progress and preferences such as sound, night mode and reduced motion locally. The Gold wallet records its balance, permanently unlocked items, reward claims and processed purchase/reward identifiers. The native app uses protected device-local Keychain storage for that wallet and local web-content storage for existing gameplay preferences; the browser wallet uses IndexedDB website storage. App and browser progress are separate.

There is no Rocket Car Landing game account, publisher-operated cloud save or cross-device wallet service. These local gameplay records are not uploaded to a game-operated server. Existing course records remain on the device. Old noncash points are retained without conversion, new awards or display; an already banked next-flight fuel bonus remains usable once. Advertising, purchases, website delivery and support involve other data as described below.

Game audio uses bundled files and locally generated effects. Core gameplay does not require access to contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

Advertising and privacy choices

The iPhone and iPad app integrates Google AdMob for paced interstitials after successfully completed ramps and optional rewarded videos. There are no banner ads. Interstitials may appear only when continuing from a successful flight report, subject to a three-completion and 120-second pacing gate; failed flights do not create an interstitial opportunity. The browser game does not load native AdMob. Ads require a network connection and availability; unavailable ads do not prevent core gameplay.

The app waits for verified native Apple purchase-entitlement information before requesting ads or advertising consent. Ad-supported players use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent information and present required privacy messages before eligible ads. When Google makes privacy options available, open Rocket Car Landing Menu → Settings → Ad privacy to reopen those choices.

An optional completed video can award 10 Gold only after the native advertising SDK confirms the earned result. The local wallet records the reward event to prevent a repeated award. Gold can unlock in-game cars but does not grant Apple Remove All Ads or legacy car purchase entitlements. A skipped, failed or unavailable video does not grant a reward. New videos do not grant old noncash points or new fuel bonuses. Remove All Ads owners see no rewarded-video offers.

Google’s advertising and consent SDKs may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, product interaction information, crash information, performance information and other diagnostics. These may be used for advertising, measurement, consent handling and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the SDK, your region, available consent choices and applicable provider settings.

For Google’s handling of this information and available controls, see Google’s privacy policy, Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures and Google My Ad Center. Consent choices do not remove all advertising or diagnostic data processing.

Apple purchases and ownership

The garage has five free Entry cars, three Mid cars at 100 Gold, five Advanced cars at 500 Gold and two Premium cars at 1,000 Gold. Gold is non-transferable, non-redeemable, non-expiring virtual currency with no cash value. The iPhone and iPad app offers three consumable Apple Gold packs when configured and available. Apple StoreKit handles checkout and provides verified product and transaction information; the wallet records the transaction identifier with delivery to prevent duplicate credit. The game does not receive your payment-card details. Apple shows the localized price before confirmation.

Gold spending and permanent item ownership are stored together in the local wallet. Existing verified purchases of the ten historical non-consumable cars continue to grant their matching cars without Gold payment. Those product identifiers remain recognized for current ownership, restoration and refunds; new car unlocks use Gold. A refunded legacy entitlement and separately earned Gold ownership are independent.

Remove All Ads remains a separate optional non-consumable Apple product. After verified current ownership, the app suppresses ad requests, interstitials and optional rewarded-video offers. An already presented SDK sheet completes normal dismissal. Removing ads grants no cars or Gold; Gold and car unlocks do not remove ads. Verified refunds or revocations remove the corresponding Apple entitlement.

Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account for current Remove All Ads and historical car entitlements. Apple Restore does not reconstruct a finished consumable balance, Gold spending, Gold-owned cars, stars or other local progress. Gold and Gold-owned items persist across normal restarts but are not guaranteed to survive app deletion, website-data clearing, device loss or migration. There is no game-operated backup or wallet synchronization service.

The browser cannot complete Apple checkout and has its own local wallet. TestFlight and sandbox transactions follow Apple’s testing rules and are separate from production purchases. Apple processes purchase and account information under Apple’s privacy policy. See our purchase and recovery guide.

Sharing is your choice.

The game creates flight-report pictures on your device using game artwork, snapshots from your actual flight, the selected city and course, and flight statistics. Choosing Share, Save PNG, or a social option opens the available system share sheet, download action or external composer.

The game does not automatically publish posts or upload contacts. If you choose to send a report, picture or link through another service, that service receives what you send and handles it under its own privacy practices. Available sharing destinations and save options depend on your device and installed apps.

Website delivery and external links

This public information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and its delivery infrastructure receive ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser headers, to deliver pages and operate the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

This information website has no advertising SDK, analytics scripts, contact form, remote font service or embedded social tracker. It uses local images and styles. The native game’s advertising SDK has its own processing as described above.

Links to Apple, Google, GitHub or another service open that provider’s website. Its own policies apply to information it receives.

Support and privacy messages

If you email or call Fang Ventures, we receive the contact information and message details you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, device information or screenshots. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Email and telephone providers also handle communications under their own policies.

Please send only information needed for your request. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information. Contact us if you have questions about information you previously sent or want to request its deletion, subject to any applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Deleting local data and device backups

Browser website-data controls can remove browser progress, Gold, ownership and preferences. Deleting the native app can remove its local game data. Keychain storage may survive some reinstall or backup workflows, but Apple does not guarantee uninstall survival; it is not a cross-device wallet backup. Device or cloud backup behavior depends on operating-system settings. Gold balances and Gold-owned items are not guaranteed recoverable after local data loss.

We do not operate a game-account database from which to recover a wallet. Deleting local data does not delete independent records held by Apple, Google, GitHub or your communications provider. Use the relevant provider’s privacy controls for those records. Verified Remove All Ads and historical car entitlements may be restored through Apple with the same Apple Account; Restore does not recover finished consumable balances, Gold-owned items or local gameplay progress.
